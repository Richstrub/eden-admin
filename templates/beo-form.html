Event information

Menu selections are due 21 days before the event. The signed BEO is due to the event manager 14 business days before the event.

The final guarantee is due by 11:00 am, 5 business days before the event. If it isn't received, the expected count becomes the minimum guarantee.

À la carte on the 3rd floor: the 3rd Floor Event Space has a $2,500 food and beverage minimum, or a $250 room fee. The room fee is on the bill below until you tick “Waive third-floor rental fee” once the minimum is met.

Event timeline

Buffets and reception stations run 90 minutes, plated dinners 120 to 180 minutes, and breaks 30 minutes. Longer service carries an additional fee.
